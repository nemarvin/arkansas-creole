Sources and cartographic conventions

Treaty / cession geometry: U.S. Government / Charles C. Royce, Indian Land Cessions in the United States, represented here by Royce numbers 94, 121, 503, and 505.

Indigenous territorial representations: Native Land Digital, Native-Land.ca. This optional layer is clipped sharply to the present-day 50 states and District of Columbia for display. Its polygons are community-informed territorial representations, not legal treaty boundaries.

Waterways: Arkansas DEQ / Arkansas GIS Office Water Base Layer. Waterways shown in the Arkansas views are clipped to the tract represented by Royce 121.

Basemap: U.S. Geological Survey, The National Map.
