Sources and cartographic conventions

Treaty geometry: U.S. Government / Charles C. Royce, Indian Land Cessions in the United States.

Native Lands: Native-Land.ca. Only territories overlapping present-day Arkansas are shown, clipped to the state boundary for display. These are community-informed representations rather than treaty boundaries.

Waterways: Arkansas GIS Office Water Base Layer.

Basemap: U.S. Geological Survey.
